Good to know

Booking questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

How far ahead can I book?

Pay-as-you-go players can book up to 3 days ahead. Members can book 3 to 14 days ahead depending on their plan.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. See our cancellation policy for the exact window and any fees.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No. Rackets, shuttles, bats and balls can be rented or bought at the front desk.

Is coaching available for beginners and kids?

Yes. Coaching courts and tables are open for group and one-to-one sessions. Ask at reception or send us a message.

Not sure which court fits?

Tell us your level and group size and we'll suggest a court.
