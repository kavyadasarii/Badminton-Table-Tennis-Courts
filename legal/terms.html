The short version: book your slot, turn up on time, play safely and treat the venue and other players with respect. The details are below.

01Accepting these terms

These terms apply when you use the Rally Point website, book a court or table, buy a membership or visit our facility at Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. By doing any of these, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy policy and Cancellation policy.

If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

02Your account

  • Give accurate details when you sign up and keep them up to date.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password private and for activity on your account.
  • Players under 18 may book and play only with a parent or guardian’s consent.
  • Accounts and memberships are personal. Please do not share or transfer them.

03Bookings & court use

  • Courts and tables are booked in one-hour slots. Availability on the website is live, but a slot is yours only once you receive a booking confirmation.
  • Please arrive on time and check in at reception. Your slot ends at its scheduled time so the next player can start on time.
  • A booking is for the named player or group and for the sport booked. Please do not resell or sublet slots.
  • Off-peak is weekdays before 5 PM. All other times are peak hours.
  • We may move or cancel a booking if a court becomes unavailable, for example for maintenance. If we do, we will offer you another slot or a refund.

04Fees, payments & refunds

Prices are shown in Indian rupees (₹). You can pay online by UPI or card, or at the front desk. Online payments are processed by a third-party payment provider.

Cancellations, rescheduling and refunds are covered in our Cancellation policy. We may change our prices at any time, but a change will not affect a booking you have already confirmed.

05Memberships

  • We offer Casual, Regular and Unlimited plans. The court time, advance-booking window, guest passes and other benefits of each plan are those shown on our Membership plans page when you join.
  • You can pay monthly, every three months or yearly. Longer billing periods may carry a discount shown at checkout.
  • On plans with included hours, peak-hour bookings use your included hours first and then member rates.
  • Eligible plans can be paused as described on the Membership plans page. You can cancel at any time. Refunds for pre-paid periods follow the Cancellation policy.
  • Guest passes are for guests playing with you and cannot be sold or transferred.

06Facility rules & conduct

  • Wear clean, non-marking indoor sports shoes on court.
  • Treat staff, other players and equipment with respect. Abuse, harassment and unsafe behaviour are not tolerated.
  • Smoking, alcohol and any illegal substances are not allowed on the premises.
  • Follow the instructions of our staff, and keep changing rooms and common areas clean.
  • Do not film or photograph other people without their permission.

07Health, safety & liability

Badminton and table tennis are physical sports and carry a risk of injury. By playing, you confirm you are fit to do so and that you take part at your own risk. If you have a medical condition, please check with your doctor first.

We look after our courts, tables and equipment with reasonable care. To the extent the law allows, Rally Point is not liable for loss of or damage to personal belongings, including items kept in lockers, or for injuries caused by your own actions or those of other players.

Nothing in these terms limits any liability that cannot be limited under Indian law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence.
