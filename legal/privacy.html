The short version: we collect only what we need to run your bookings and memberships, we never sell your personal data, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time.

01Who we are

Rally Point (“we”, “us”) runs indoor badminton courts and table tennis tables at Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use our website, book a court, join a membership or visit the facility.

By using our services you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or our booking services.

02Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address and password.
  • Booking and membership details: the courts and slots you book, your plan, billing period and the names of any guests you bring.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded and a transaction reference. Card and UPI credentials are handled by our payment provider, not by us.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Device and usage data: browser type, pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • On-site data: we may use CCTV in common areas of the facility for safety and security. We do not film in changing rooms.
  • Photos and videos: only at events, and only where you have agreed to be featured.

03How we use it

  • Create your account, confirm bookings and manage your membership.
  • Process payments, refunds and billing.
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders and important service messages.
  • Answer your questions and provide support.
  • Keep the facility, players and staff safe, and prevent misuse of our services.
  • Improve the website, court availability and the way we run the venue.
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We send promotional messages, such as offers and event invitations, only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time using the link in the message or by contacting us.

04Payments

Online payments by UPI or card are processed by a third-party payment gateway. We do not see or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Payments made at the front desk are recorded against your booking or membership.

05Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run Rally Point, such as payment gateways, SMS and email services and website hosting. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities when the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our players and staff.
  • A new owner, if Rally Point is ever transferred or merged, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data.

06Cookies & local storage

Our website uses a small amount of storage in your browser to work properly. For example, we remember your light or dark theme and your text-direction choice. We may also use basic analytics to understand which pages are useful.

You can clear this data or block cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, but your preferences will not be remembered.

07How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide our services. Some records, such as invoices and payment history, must be kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law. After that we delete or anonymise the data.

08Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation.
  • Stop sending you marketing messages, or withdraw any consent you have given.
  • Respond to a complaint about how your data has been handled.

To use any of these rights, email play@rallypoint.in. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

09Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data, including secure connections (HTTPS) and limited staff access. No online service can be completely risk-free, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

10Children

Players under 18 may book and play only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child without a parent or guardian’s consent. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will remove it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell you by email or on the website.
