The short version: cancel at least 24 hours before your slot for a full refund, tell us early if you can’t make it, and if we ever cancel on you, you get another slot or your money back.

01Overview

This policy applies to court and table bookings, memberships, guest passes and equipment rentals at Rally Point, Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. It forms part of our Terms & conditions.

All times are counted from the start time of your booked slot, in Indian Standard Time.

02Cancelling a booking

The refund you receive depends on how far ahead you cancel:

  • 24 hours or more before your slot: full refund.
  • Between 6 and 24 hours before: 50% refund.
  • Less than 6 hours before: no refund.

Bookings made with included membership hours are not charged, so nothing is refunded. Cancelling in time returns those hours to your plan.

03Rescheduling

You can move a booking to another slot free of charge up to 12 hours before it starts, subject to availability. If the new slot costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method.

Rescheduling later than 12 hours before the slot is treated as a cancellation and a new booking.

04If we cancel

Sometimes a court or table becomes unavailable, for example because of maintenance, a power or equipment problem, or a safety issue. If we cancel or have to move your booking, you can choose:

  • another slot at no extra cost, or
  • a full refund of what you paid, or
  • the hours returned to your plan, if you booked using membership hours.

If play is cut short by a problem on our side, we will refund or credit the time you lost.

05No-shows & late arrivals

  • If you do not turn up and have not cancelled, the booking is treated as a no-show and is not refunded. Membership hours used for a no-show are not returned.
  • Your slot ends at its scheduled time, even if you arrive late, so the next player can start on time.
  • If you have not checked in 15 minutes after your start time and we have not heard from you, we may release the court to another player. No refund is due in that case.

06Cancelling a membership

You can cancel your Casual, Regular or Unlimited plan at any time. Your benefits continue until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that, the plan does not renew.

  • Within 48 hours of joining, if you have not made a booking: full refund.
  • Monthly plans: no refund for the current month once the 48-hour window has passed.
  • Three-month and yearly plans: we refund the full months you have not used. Unused months are calculated at the standard monthly price, so any discount you received for paying in advance is deducted.

Bookings you have already made for dates after your plan ends are treated as normal bookings and follow the rules above.

07Pausing a membership

Eligible plans can be paused instead of cancelled. The pause length, how often you can pause and which plans qualify are set out on our Membership plans page. A paused plan is not refunded; the time simply moves to when you resume.

08Guest passes & rentals

  • A guest pass that has been used is not refunded. An unused pass can be returned to your plan if you cancel the booking it was attached to in time.
  • Unused guest passes cannot be sold or transferred and end with your plan.
  • Equipment rentals can be cancelled free of charge before you collect them. Once collected, rental fees are not refunded.

09How refunds are paid

  • Refunds for online payments go back to the UPI account or card you paid with. They normally reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Refunds for payments made at the front desk are returned by UPI transfer or cash at the desk, or as credit on your account if you prefer.
  • We do not charge a fee for processing a refund.

If a refund has not arrived after 7 working days, please contact us with your booking or transaction reference and we will follow it up with our payment provider.

10How to cancel

  • Log in to your account and open your dashboard, then choose the booking or plan you want to change.
  • Call or message us on +91 90000 00000.
  • Email play@rallypoint.in with your name and the booking details.

The time we receive your request is the time that counts, so cancelling on the website is the fastest way to be sure it is recorded.

11Exceptions

If you cannot play because of an injury, a medical emergency or a bereavement, please tell us as soon as you can. We will look at your situation fairly and may offer a refund, credit or new slot even where the rules above would not.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. A change does not affect bookings you have already confirmed or plans you have already paid for.
