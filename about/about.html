Our story

Started with a missed booking

Rally Point began with a simple frustration: turning up to a court only to find it taken, or calling three places to find one free hour. We thought playing should be the easy part.

So we built a club where availability is visible, booking takes a minute, and the courts are the kind you actually look forward to: proper wooden floors, glare-free lighting and tables a league player would trust.

Today the hall is busy from early morning to late night, and the best part is still the same: people walking in, picking up a racket and having a good time.

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our mission

To make playing badminton and table tennis simple, affordable and welcoming for everyone in Hyderabad, with great courts, honest pricing and a booking experience that gets out of your way.

Our vision

A city where racket sports are part of everyday life: where a school kid, an office team and a weekend veteran all have a court close by, and a community to rally with.

Our values

How we run the place

Everyone plays

All ages, all levels. No cliques, no pressure, just a court and a good game.

Zero friction

Live availability and a 60-second booking. Less admin, more playing.

Quality first

Maintained floors, proper lighting and tournament-grade tables, every day.

Community

Leagues, socials and coaching that turn regulars into friends.

Our journey

Milestones so far

From a single hall to a full club, one rally at a time.

  1. Jan 2026

    First serve

    The Jubilee Hills hall opens with four badminton courts and two tables, and the first league night sells out.

  2. Apr 2026

    No more phone calls

    Live court availability and one-minute booking go online, so a free hour is always one tap away.

  3. Jul 2026

    Play more, pay less

    Casual, Regular and Unlimited plans arrive, along with weekly coaching clinics for juniors and beginners.

  4. Oct 2026

    Room to grow

    The hall expands to 8 badminton courts and 6 tables, with extra evening slots for office teams.

Up next: inter-club tournaments, a members' leaderboard and early-morning school programmes.
