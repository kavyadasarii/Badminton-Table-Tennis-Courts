Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Our front desk is happy to help, whether it's about booking, memberships or what to bring.

Do I need to bring my own racket and shuttles?

No. Rackets and shuttlecocks are available to rent at the front desk, or you can bring your own.

How long is a booking slot?

Slots are one hour. You can book back-to-back slots if the court is free after yours.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes. Cancel or move your slot free of charge up to a few hours before it starts. See our cancellation policy for details.

Is there parking and changing rooms?

Yes. There is on-site parking, plus changing rooms and lockers for members and walk-ins.

Can I pause my membership?

Regular and Unlimited members can freeze their plan when travelling or injured and pick up again later.
