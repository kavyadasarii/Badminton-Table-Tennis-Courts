A day at Rally Point

There's a rhythm for everyone

  1. 6 – 9 AM

    Early birds

    Quiet courts, cool air and the best light of the day. Ideal for solo drills and coaching.

  2. 12 – 4 PM

    Off-peak calm

    Wide-open availability and the friendliest rates for members who like space.

  3. 5 – 9 PM

    Prime time

    Leagues, doubles and full courts. The hall is at its loudest and best.

  4. 9 – 11 PM

    Late sessions

    Wind down with relaxed games, then recover in the lounge before heading home.

Good to know

Before you visit

Equipment

Rackets, shuttles and paddles are available to rent at reception. Please wear non-marking indoor shoes.

Parking

On-site parking for cars and two-wheelers, free for the duration of your booking.

Guests

Guests can join any booking for a small day fee.Members get complimentary guest passes.

Cancellations

Cancel up to four hours before your slot for a full credit. Read the policy.
